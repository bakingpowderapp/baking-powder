Baking Powder conventions; find returns lines holding every word. Key definitions: manual with path or row.

Presets: A track preset loads its rack and LFO lanes; fields written beside it override. A rack row preset loads with its LFO lanes; sibling keys override. Example: {"preset":"Effects/Freak/Sear.json","wet":0.85}. A preset takes the signal from no track: a gate, sidechain or space row in it arrives without sourceTrack, or keeps the one of the row it replaces; write sourceTrack beside it to give one.
Presets rows: LFO lanes and curves written beside a track preset name rows from the preset's own first row, 0, and land where those rows do; the reply tells of any dropped for want of its row or knob. A rack written beside it replaces the preset's rack: every row then counts in the written rack, and the preset's own lanes land on it by the same numbers or are dropped silently.
Presets: A path written at tracks/T/preset replaces the sound, keeping name, clips and, when the preset has no midi row, the track's own; curves stay on their row numbers, so one may now move another row's knob of that name, and one whose row lacks its knob is dropped unsaid; with no Core, or on an audio or group track, its effects join the end instead. A row's /preset replaces that row and its LFO lanes, keeping curves on keys the new row has; the reply tells of any dropped. rack/end adds one. To change a loaded knob, add a knob edit after it in the same edits list.
Presets saving: save with preset, a path in the song, and path, a new .json under a browser folder, writes a preset as the app's save panel does and leaves the song as it is: a rack row's path, as tracks/0/rack/2, saves that row with its LFO lanes; tracks/0, returns/0 or master saves the whole rack with them; and effects true beside a track's path saves its effects alone, the midi and Core rows left out. description is needed, twelve words at most saying what the sound is, never naming gear, a brand or how it was made; library shows it and find searches it. A file already at path refuses unless overwrite true is written and the file is itself a preset.
Midi rows: A MIDI track's rack opens with a midi row, fixed at row 0, whose arp feeds the Cores below it; a midi row further down plays what the midi row above plays into the Cores below it in turn, as far as a bus row: below one the chain starts again from row 0. Sequence presets carry them; set arp/on false when written notes give the rhythm; arp/presetLock true keeps the row's arp when a preset lands. Row 0's velocity, 100 by default, scales every note the track plays, 127 playing them as written; a preset keeps the track's, and a lower midi row's does nothing. transposeSemitones moves every note entering a midi row up or down, 24 at most, before its arp; a preset keeps row 0's, and a lower midi row's moves what the row above plays. forceKey true moves each note a midi row sends on to the nearest note of the song's root and scale, after its arp; every note outside the scale sits halfway between two, so ties picks which: up, down, alternate, which takes turns from up and starts over whenever playback starts or loops, or smart, which keeps each note's interval above the lowest held key, so a chord shape, a stack or a STEP line lands on the key's own. A preset keeps a track's forceKey and ties once on. A new Core sounds only osc1; noise follows ampEnv.
Follows: a track with follows N also plays the notes leaving tracks/N's last midi row, arp and all, as they play, live keys included, through its own midi rows from row 0 on, with its own notes; its velocity scales them. The last midi row is the lowest in the rack, so with a bus row it is bus 2's when bus 2 holds one. A follower loses its notes when the track it follows is flattened to audio or removed.
Buses: a bus row splits a MIDI track's rack in two: the rows above it are bus 1, the rows below bus 2, each with its own Cores and effects, summed at the row's levels, bus 1 then bus 2; nothing follows the sum, and a rack holds one bus row at most. Row 0's midi row feeds both buses; a midi row inside a bus plays what row 0 plays into that bus's Cores below it and none of the other bus's. The app shows two tabs, one for each bus, with row 0's midi row the first row beneath either.
Core waves: each oscillator has four wave slots; shapes names the slot shapes (default saw, square, triangle, sine, any of the 16 in manual), levels their levels; a lane's param reaches a slot's level as osc1.levels[0] to osc1.levels[3], the app's WAVE 1 to WAVE 4. The shape drawn plays the oscillator's points, one cycle, as a drawn LFO's.
Core analog: analog holds drift, a slow random wander of pitch, cutoff and pulse width; scatter, a random offset drawn for each note; heat, saturation in each voice; and a low cut; bypass true switches them all off. driftCents is the per-note tune scatter and wanderCents the slow drift. Pulse width moves only on an oscillator sounding square alone.
Core seed: a Core row's seed picks the run of random draws its drift and scatter take. Left out, the row's place gives one; the row then keeps it when it or its track is moved, or others are inserted or removed above it, so its sound stays. A copy or a newly written Core takes its own, and no two Cores share one.

Notes: Compact form is [pitch,startBeat,lengthBeats,velocity], velocity 127 when left out; startBeat is clip-relative, other beats song-relative. Clip starts and lengths round to 1/960 beat; note beats are kept as written. Clips don't loop; notes outside lengthBeats are silent. Don't overlap a track's clips; copy repeats passages.
Notes filters: transpose, scale, fit and quantize take fromBeat and untilBeat (song beats, by note start), above and below (pitches, exclusive) and picked true (the notes picked in the app's open clip, its path as path); any filter leaves waves alone. scale on a curve takes fromBeat and untilBeat for its nodes.
Fit: write fit [lowest, highest] at a clip, a track or, with no path, the whole song moves each note outside the range by octaves to the nearest pitch inside, pitch classes kept, and drops a note landing on another at its pitch and start. Keep the bass with above; preview lists the pitches that would move, 40 paths at most.
Transpose song: write transpose with no path moves every note, and root with them, so the key label follows, as transposing in the app does; waves stay as they are. A wave is transposed at its own clip or track, and without warp it changes speed with its pitch. With a filter, the chosen notes alone move and root stays.
Patterns: A pattern object written at a clip's notes tiles across lengthBeats as plain notes; after lengthening the clip, write it again. Example: {"steps":"x...x...x...x...","pitch":35}.
Patterns steps: x hits, X accents, . rests, - extends the note one step. ~ after x, X or its last - takes no step and slides to the next hit over Core's mono/glideMs without retriggering. Whitespace is ignored.
Patterns timing: stepBeats defaults to 0.25, noteBeats to stepBeats; both above 0. swing is 0–0.5 of a step, delaying every second step. Each - adds stepBeats to the note.
Patterns pitches: Give pitch or pitches, never both. pitches is a chord [57,60,64] or chords [[57,60,64],[53,57,60]] changing every chordBeats (default 4). arp true cycles one pitch per hit; a flat pitches list then plays as a repeating line.
Patterns velocity: velocity is 1–127 (default 100) or [from,to] ramped across the clip; accent is X's velocity (default 127).

Tracks names: a track written without a name, or with an empty one, is named as the app names a new track, its kind and the next number, as MIDI 3; the reply gives the name it took.
Tracks insert: Insert renumbers every sourceTrack and follows; the incoming track's references use pre-insert indices. Without inGroup, it joins the preceding group or member's group, unless itself a group. A group cannot land among another group's members.
Tracks remove: null written at tracks/N removes the track and renumbers every sourceTrack and follows, dropping those that named it; a group's members stay, ungrouped. A song keeps one track at least.
Tracks move: write move M at tracks/N puts the track at tracks/M, as dragging it in the app: a group takes its members, and every sourceTrack and follows is renumbered. Landing right under a group or one of its members it joins that group; inGroup false written after leaves it out, below the group. A group cannot land among another group's members.
Tracks ungroup: inGroup false written at a member's own path moves it below its group, as UNGROUP in the app, so the members after it stay grouped; the reply gives its new index. To ungroup several, write the last member first, so none moves; null at the group's own path removes the group and ungroups them all. A write that would leave a track marked inGroup with no group above it is refused.
Tracks flatten: FLATTEN in the app's header turns each picked audio or MIDI track into audio, a group's member included, and never a group; it renders sound, so the user presses it. A track following one it turns into audio loses its notes, unless it is picked too.
Rack rows: write insert with a row object at a rack row puts it there, a row preset included, and move M at a rack row takes it to row M; null there removes it. The rows between shift and their curves and LFO lanes follow; those on a removed row go with it. A rack holds 40 rows, a row counting once for each of its times; a write that would overfill one is refused whole and says by how many rows. A midi or Core row whose times changes while notes sound lets them finish as they were; the new count plays from the next note. A MIDI track keeps its midi row at row 0 and one Core at least. With a bus row, a row above it plays in bus 1 and one below in bus 2. A midi row plays only the Cores below it in its bus, so insert one above them, at row 1 for bus 1 or right under the bus row for bus 2, where the app's + puts it; rack/end leaves it under them all, playing none.
Folded values: A folded string stands for what its path holds when it is written, a library MIDI part's notes included; write resolves it inside a value, or as the whole value at its own key: notes, clips, rack, curves, lfos or palette. If the count, range or names it gives no longer match, write refuses: read again. Any other change since the read is taken as it stands; unchangedSince catches one.
Write replies: revision, counts and operation outcomes; details true adds paths, project totals and each confirmation. Values kept differently, preset pitch offsets, silent clips and overlaps always show. On a value kept differently, sent is what was typed there, written what a preset or an operation put there. unchangedSince refuses only when the written paths moved, or what a preset loaded or a rack, row or kind written there replaces; other changes are counted.
Write replies on notes: each clip whose notes changed gets a Notes line: notes changed, added or removed, pitch range, bars touched, pitch classes and timing kept or changed, then its chords. It counts the track's sounding notes by song beat, so notes carried into another clip at their beats count as kept. read gives chords beside every clip's notes, by clip beat; a clip's key is the song's root and scale whenever its notes fit them nearly as well as any key, with the notes outside counted. With the track's arp, transposeSemitones or forceKey on, a lane on a midi row's knob, or follows, the summary ends with what its midi rows play into its Cores over the clip, as playback plays them, both buses together, described the same way, so a pattern's line and its key are read without flattening.
Copies: copies counts copies after the source passage, so copies 2 plays it three times; the reply and preview give the total and bars. Each copy replaces the clips that lay there, the source's silence included. Curves, those on LFO settings too, repeat with each copy, so a ramp written across the source restarts on every copy; for a rise across a whole section, copy first, then write one curve from the section's first beat to its last.
Join: write join, beats from fromBeat, at tracks/N or the whole song merges each track's clips there into one, notes shifted, as ⌘J on a loop in the app; audio joins only when its clips play on from one wave, else the user joins them in the app.
Join clips: write join with a list of MIDI clip paths, one a track and no path, stacks them into one clip on the topmost of those tracks, as ⌘J on clips picked across tracks: the clip spans them all and takes in that track's other clips there, a note overlapping a longer one at its pitch is dropped, and the lower clips stay.
Duplicate: write duplicate true at tracks/N inserts a copy after the track, a group with its members, references renumbered, as ⌘D in the app; a copied row taking the signal from a track keeps taking it, unless that track takes in the copy's sound, through a group or a signal, so the signal would loop, and then it arrives without sourceTrack. At tracks/N/clips/K the clip plays again right after itself, over whatever lay there, its automation carried unless automationLock.
Cut: write cut B, or a list of beats, at tracks/N splits the clip under each beat in two, as holding a clip does in the app; with no path every track is cut. Pieces of one wave play on unbroken until one is moved, removed or changed.
Slicing: to slice a wave clip at its hits, listen to its track alone, then cut about 3 ms before each hit's beat, 0.006 beat at 120 bpm, since a clip's edge fades over 1.5 ms. Drop the pieces not wanted by writing null at their paths, last first. To put a hit on a grid line, start its piece that same 3 ms before the line, not on it, and shorten the piece before it. warp false on a piece of a warped clip keeps its start and plays the rest at the file's own speed; where the file runs faster than the song, cut the piece's lengthBeats to bpm over warpBpm of what it was, or it plays on into the file's next hit. hz is a clue, not a name: compare a clip's hits with one another, lower for its kicks and higher for its hats, as the figures shift from one recording to the next. A gate row silences what lies between hits and an eq row trims what bleeds.
Flatten: an arp is a note maker, and write flatten true at a midi row, tracks/N/rack/R, with its arp on, prints the notes it sends as playback plays them, every timing, gate, tie, slide, velocity, cents, mod and shift, its curves and LFO lanes heard, into the track's clips as written notes, then switches the arp off and sets velocity 127 and transposeSemitones 0, as FLATTEN on the row's face, so what plays is unchanged. Clips grow to hold every note whole and join where a note would cross into the next; follows is dropped, as the print holds the followed notes. The midi rows feeding the row are set the same way, their forceKey off too, and the curves and LFO lanes on all of them are dropped, as the print holds what they did. When a Core or the last midi row lies outside the row's own chain, as with a Core above it, the row instead moves with the Cores and midi rows below it onto a new track right after, which takes copies of the effects below it and the bus's level and which the reply names; tracks following this one follow the new track when it holds their notes. A row with no Core below it moves onto a new muted track with a stock Core. Flatten refuses, with the words the row's FLAT cell shows, exactly when it would change what plays: the arp is off or the row bypassed, the song holds 64 tracks and the row would move, a copied effect other than an eq, utility, delay or reverb would part sound a Core above also feeds, a copied effect's LFO lane restarting on notes would part that sound, a volume or send LFO lane restarts on notes, or another row takes this track's signal. A sidechain row's FLATTEN and a track's FLATTEN to audio render sound, so the user presses those in the app; flattening a track to audio leaves the tracks following it without notes.
Quantize: write quantize G, a grid in beats as 0.25 for sixteenths, at a MIDI clip, a track or, with no path, the whole song snaps each note's start and end to the nearest grid line, no note left shorter than one grid step, as quantize in the piano roll; fromBeat, untilBeat, above, below and picked choose the notes, and a note the piano roll does not show, below C0, above B7 or starting past its clip's end, stays as it is. A note that lands on another of its pitch takes the part of it that it covers, as in the piano roll, and the reply counts the notes that gave way.
Double and halve: write double true at a MIDI clip doubles its length, its notes stretching with it, and is refused when the next clip on the track leaves no room; halve true halves a clip of two beats or more, its notes shrinking with it; as the piano roll's times two and halve.
Reverse notes: write reverse true at a MIDI clip turns its notes back to front in time, the last heard first, ties and slides staying between the same pairs of notes; a note starting past the clip's end is dropped, as reverse in the piano roll.
Chords from notes: write chords true at a MIDI clip replaces its notes with chords, the notes each bar strikes gathered into one chord held until the next changes, as the piano roll's chords action; an arp's line becomes the chords it spells.
Fades: a wave's fadeIn and fadeOut shape its level in file seconds, as the two pairs of nodes in the wave view: fadeIn is silent before its fromSeconds and rises to full level at its toSeconds, fadeOut is full until its fromSeconds and falls to silence at its toSeconds, and bend bows either. One shorter than 5 ms plays over 5 ms, fadeIn ending at its toSeconds and fadeOut starting at its fromSeconds. The two never cross: fadeIn's toSeconds may equal fadeOut's fromSeconds and go no further. A fade written across the other stops where they meet, the later of two edits at the earlier, and the reply gives what was kept; both in one value and crossing, fadeOut gives way. A cut leaves both on each piece, which sounds as it did.
Match clips: write match true at tracks/N/clips/K gives that audio clip's fadeIn, fadeOut, level spans, semitones and cents to every clip on its track that sounds like it, each from its own start and fadeOut from its own end, starting no sooner than the clip, as ALL in the wave view; clips are alike when their lengths are within four times and their loudness shape and brightness are close, whatever their files are called. The reply names the clips that took them.
Match sidechains: write match true at a sidechain row gives its settings, preEffects with them, to every other sidechain row with the same sourceTrack, on tracks, returns and master, as MATCH ALL in the app; each keeps its own wet, bypass and preview.
Clips and curves: a clip removed or moved by writing its path leaves the curves under it where they are; copy and duplicate carry them unless automationLock, which stops no lane written by path.
Revisions: Numbers come from reads, previews, listens and writes, never from the user's edits in the app; a whole read holds its number as revision, a path read gives it as a second text after the value. Every read shows those edits at once, and unchangedSince compares the song itself, so it refuses on them before any number moves. read with revision R gives a path as it stood then, among the last 32 kept, so an original stays reachable while alternatives are tried; write it back to restore it.
Preview: write with preview true reports the operations, the first 40 paths that would change, each with what it holds and what it would hold, then a count of the rest, and the revision to pass as unchangedSince; a list changing length shows as one path. Nothing is written, and wave paths are refused.

Automation gain: A gain curve replaces the fader all song, holding its first and last values outside its nodes; scale it to adjust. A gain LFO also replaces the fader, center 0.67 by default.
Automation sends: A curve or LFO with send S instead of row and param replaces tracks/T/sendLevels/S all song; read it there with fromBeat and untilBeat.
Automation nodes: Duplicate beats keep only the first node. For a jump, separate nodes by one tick (1/960 beat). Interpolation follows knob travel, so cutoffHz moves logarithmically. A node takes bar in place of beat and, on a gain lane, valueDb in place of value; reads give beat and value.
Automation bend: Above 0 bows a segment toward the higher value, so a rise moves early and a fall late; below 0 bows it toward the lower value, so a rise moves late and a fall early. An accelerating rise is bend -1, an accelerating fall 1. Inverting a curve's values flips every bend's sign; reversing it in time keeps them.
Reverb reverseLevel: The swell starts about decayS before a note, peaks at its start and ends with it. For a swell ending on a downbeat, use a short note ending there and forwardLevel 0.

Listening: The master clamps at full scale; overDb reports clipping that peakDb 0 hides. The user hears only play. A solo listen stays ducked or gated by its sidechain signal.
Listening signals: a gate, sidechain or space row taking the signal from a track hears all of it whether that track is muted, silenced by a solo elsewhere or has one Core heard alone: before its effects with preEffects true, after its effects and fader without. A group's signal is its own tracks through its rack, leaving out those muted themselves, whatever is soloed inside the group or out and even while the group is muted.
Listening meters: lufs -120 is silence; sideDb is side relative to mid. bandsDb is each band's share of energy: sub 40–120, lowMid 120–400, mid 400–1600, high 1600–6400, top 6400–16000 Hz.
Listening spans: Mix spans report lufs, peakDb, peakBeat and hits, track and return spans lufs; edges fall on 10 ms hops. dynamics reports maximum reduction, or a space or suppressor row's deepest cut, per span too.
Listening hits: each hit is [beat, peakDb, hz]: beat where its attack starts, the first sample 6 dB over the 30 ms before it and above -60 dB; peakDb the loudest sample of the 50 ms around it, rises there merged; hz how bright those 50 ms are, the pitch of a steady tone as bright, low for a kick, high for a hat. peakBeat is the loudest instant. A span under 0.4 s reports ungated loudness as lufs; bandsDb comes from any length. Without spans the whole render lists its hits. A list holds 32 hits at most, moreHits counting the rest.
Listening details: spans replace bars; dynamics skips rows with no reduction. details true keeps bars and every row, and adds meter placement. A mix listen always includes tracks and returns; flags shows when any fire.
Listening one Core: listen with track N and row R renders one Core row of that track alone, as soloing a Core in the rack does: the other Cores of the track are silent and its effects are passed over, or kept with effects true; a track with several Cores is taken apart this way, one listen for each, while a row elsewhere taking that track's signal still hears every Core. Soloing a send in the app is listen with return.
Listening returns: returns lists each sounding return, measured after its rack and gain. return N renders one return alone, fed by every send, with no dry sound.
Modulation: read with fromBeat and untilBeat at a knob or a gain samples what plays, curves and LFOs combined, with min and max. Lanes that start over on notes hear the notes leaving the midi row above, as playback does; lag is left out.
Modulation sources: sources lists the lanes in the samples, each by path; inside lists a Core row's own LFOs, envelopes, key tracking, velocity, modulator oscillator, note mod, drift and scatter on the key, which move per note or voice and are not sampled.
Listening playback: A render leaves playback running; revision names the document it measured. While playing is true the user is listening: leave play alone unless asked to move the playhead.
Listening null: against R also renders revision R, soloed alike, and subtracts it sample by sample; null reports the difference: peakDb and lufs, -120 a full null, peakBeat its loudest instant, differs the [fromBeat, untilBeat] ranges, by bar, that hold any, 16 at most with moreDiffers counting the rest, and spans each span's peakDb. The same song renders the same samples; a change sounds on through tails, sidechains and the master, so differs can outlast the edit.
Listening difference: difference with a path keeps what against subtracts as a .wav for the user to hear, named wav under null: this render less that revision's, as path keeps the render, and a listen keeps one of the two. It holds everything that changed, moved timing, pitch and level as well as what was taken out, so to hear only what a cleanup removed, null against a revision that differs by the cleanup alone.
Listening flags: "at one loudness" means adjacent spans differ by under 2 LU as tracks enter or leave. Renders are cached per document and outer span, so new spans reuse them.

Library paths: Entries are relative to their folder; join the two. A path may start with a browser folder's name. library with add true and path asks the user to allow that folder in the app, once; save, export and move then reach it and everything under it. Only the user can re-add a missing folder.
Hearts: heart marks a file the way the browser's heart does, never a folder; hearted false clears it. Each browser folder keeps its hearts in a Hearts.json at its top, the Factory folders in one kept outside them, so they outlive the session. library entries carry hearted true, and library with hearted true lists only those, without walking the folders.
Files: write imports a clip's wave.path WAV, as written. import's ready clip is warped, with warpBpm, when the file's name holds a tempo from 60 to 200 or its length is 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 bars at a tempo near the song's, as a wave dropped in the app. A library MIDI part is a writable clip. save without path writes the project's own file, even outside browser folders; copy true writes a standalone copy, the project keeping its file. save and export make missing folders. A .wav of the whole song lets its tail ring out; a loop, or a span ending before the song does, is cut at its end. export with a clip object writes a .mid without touching the song; clip as a list, a track path or nothing writes one MIDI track per clip or track, timed from fromBeat or the earliest clip.
Export clip: export with a .wav path and clip, one audio clip's path, writes that clip alone as heard, its trims, pitch, warp, level spans, fadeIn and fadeOut in the sound and its track's rack left out, as SAVE in the wave view; fromBeat and untilBeat, song beats, crop it, and a sound past full scale is lowered to fit, the reply saying by how much. listen with track and path keeps a track's sound through its rack.
Sessions: After the app reopens, or the user opens another project, earlier revisions are gone; read before writing again.
Large writes: A script can POST application/json to the MCP URL (default http://127.0.0.1:9393/mcp), no token or session: {"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"tools/call","params":{"name":"write","arguments":{"edits":[]}}}.
